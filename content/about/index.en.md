I'm Clément Le Priol. I make things: stage lights, circuit boards, tools for my projects… and sometimes all of them at once.

## The stage

I perform with the improv theatre troupe **les Acides Animés**. I quickly got drawn to what happens behind the lighting desk: for almost eight years now I've been running our show lights with [QLC+](https://www.qlcplus.org/), an open-source lighting console I've contributed to by sharing controller profiles.

Every show is a good excuse to dream up a new piece of lighting: [light poles]({{< ref "/diy/poteaux-lumineux-v1" >}}) for an improv wrestling match, [battery-powered LED lanterns]({{< ref "/diy/lanternes-leds" >}})…

## Electronics

ESP32 microcontrollers, addressable LED strips, WLED, DMX, RS485: I love understanding how things work and bending them to my needs. I've also built projects around electric skateboards and lithium-ion batteries, which you'll find on [GitHub](https://github.com/Peemouse).

## Why this site?

Because I take so much inspiration from what other makers share, I document my own projects in return: the wins, the struggles and the tricks. If one of them sparks an idea, [drop me a line]({{< ref "/contact" >}})!
